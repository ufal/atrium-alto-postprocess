## Context

The issue was opened as a 2010+ categorisation complaint. The cause is `rule_short_garbage` (gate 5 in `determine_category`), which returns `Trash` unconditionally for any 1–3 token line without Czech diacritics, weak language ID and any weirdness. It is the largest single Trash producer — 6,333 firings, 28.4% of all Trash lines — and it fires hardest on post-2000 documents because those are tabular, one value per cell.

@david-spacil proposes gating it behind `_has_strong_garbage_evidence()`, the same predicate six section-9 rules use. Measured over both collections that lifts 378,735 lines and demotes none, at roughly 76% correct — but promotes ~26,000 genuine garbage lines to `Clear` (the top category, not a reviewable middle state) and fails one regression test. `tools/SWEEP_NOTES.md` classifies the rule `LOAD-BEARING`.

Two framings were tried and abandoned before the current one. The **symmetry argument** (that the rule is the seventh sibling of six gated rules) does not hold: `50472fb` added `qs < thresh_trash` to sites that were already gated and never touched this rule, which sits among eight *early-exit* rules that all return unconditionally. And **§6's "378,735 lifted / 0 demoted"** is a theorem rather than a measurement — deleting an unconditional `return Trash` is monotone by construction — so the only evidence about whether the change is *right* is the 508-line grading.

## Research

**1. What separates the two populations at 1–2 tokens.**
Nothing the pipeline currently computes. `vansasaasasa` and `malakofauna` agree on valid-word ratio (1.000), weird ratio (0.350), garbage density (0.000), gibberish (0), ghost share (0) and raw quality score (0.647 vs 0.647). A quality-score gate was built and measured: dead, because raw pre-alignment scores overlap completely (garbage 0.52–0.65, notation 0.49–0.86, with `II/C` and `I-VIII-c` *below* most garbage).

**2. Whether perplexity can arbitrate.**
No, and it runs backwards. Both surviving routes gate on *low* language-ID confidence; FastText scores `oueussd` at 0.9163 and `malakofauna` at 0.56. With the cap lifted, real notation demotes at perplexity 3,000 while `oueussd` survives to 30,000. @david-spacil's recomputed values corroborate: `II/C` ≈ 6·10⁷ (correct) against `oueussd` ≈ 4,600 (garbage).

**3. Whether the existing structural protections could be widened.**
`is_structured_line()` dispatches to seven predicates; all seven returned `False` on all 35 thread examples (245 evaluations, zero matches). But widening *that* predicate is the wrong lever — it is read at eleven places and is an absolute veto at the first statement of `_has_strong_garbage_evidence()`, so widening it would also disable `rule_allcaps`, `rule_garbage_density`, `rule_zero_alpha`, `rule_vowelless` and `rule_mid_uppercase`. Hence a separate, narrower predicate.

**4. Whether the wc/char boundary can isolate the problem lines.**
No. @david-spacil's error rates are flat — 31% / 28% / 25% by token count, 17–42% by character band with no monotone trend — and the tightest `wc <= 3, chars <= 24` box encloses 482 of 508 lines, 343 of them correct. `sektlll` and `edelite` (wrong) sit in the same band as `Kaaden` and `Pinii` (right). Note this reads as a reversal of his earlier "shorter is riskier, monotonically"; the two reconcile (ratio excluding borderline vs error rate including it) but the September `SHORT_PPL_CAP` argument rested on the gradient being real.

## Algorithm sketch — where a short line is decided now

1. **Section 1** — three perplexity routes. Notation is exempt from `rule_extreme_ppl` and `rule_absolute_ppl`; `rule_hard_sweep` still applies when FastText independently failed to place the line.
2. **Sections 2–4** — inverted scan, all-caps, garbage density. Unchanged. Notation is not immune here: `Reg.Bez.Aussig.` sits at `rot_ratio` 0.583 against a 0.65 threshold, `II/C` at `garbage_density` 0.25 against 0.35.
3. **Gate 5 `rule_short_garbage`** — skipped when `forgiven`, `structured`, or `is_domain_notation()`.
4. **Gate 7 `rule_short_line`** — total for `wc <= 2`; every branch returns, so `check_rescues()` in section 9 is unreachable for this population. A notation line that survives to here returns `Clear`, with `categorize_line` clamping its score *up* to `CATEG_NOISY_SCORE_MAX` (0.80). `II/C` goes from a raw 0.4792 to a pinned 0.80.

Step 4 is the honest cost of D6, and it is the same "promotes to the top category, not a reviewable middle state" objection raised against the contributor's own patch. It applies here verbatim and should be stated rather than buried.
