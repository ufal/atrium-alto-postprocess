## 🎯 What the issue was, and what shipped

The issue was opened as "the 2010+ documents are categorised badly". The cause was one rule.

`rule_short_garbage` (gate 6 of `determine_category`, `text_util.py:1353-1371`) returned `Trash` **unconditionally** for any 1–3 token line with no Czech diacritics, weak language ID and any weirdness. It was the largest single `Trash` producer in the corpus — 6,333 firings, **28.4% of all `Trash` lines**, 62.5% on the two documents @DanaKriv flagged. It concentrates on post-2000 documents for a structural reason: those documents are tabular, and one value per cell is precisely its firing profile.

The merged change adds the second witness its six §9 neighbours already required:

```python
_fire("rule_short_garbage")
if qs < CATEG_TRASH_SCORE_MAX + 0.35 and _has_strong_garbage_evidence(...):
    return "Trash", "trash_threshold"
```

Two hunks, `text_util.py` +11 / −1, plus the fixture updates that record the flip as a diff rather than a claim.

No monotone trend in either dimension. The tightest `wc ≤ w, chars ≤ c` box holding 95% of the errors is **`3, 24`** — and it encloses 482 of the 508 lines, 343 of them correct. `sektlll` and `edelite` (wrong) sit in the same band as `Kaaden` and `Pinii` (right).

**This is why the narrowing had to be a shape predicate and not a word-count or character boundary.** The question "can we draw a box around the problematic short lines?" was asked directly in the thread and answered in the negative by measurement.

An earlier 3.6 / 5.1 / 6.8 : 1 correct-to-wrong gradient by token count is not a "shorter is riskier" law either: at `wc == 3` the `SHORT_PPL_CAP` no longer applies, raw perplexity reaches the sweep, and garbage still gets caught. It is a constant interaction, not a property of short lines.

By decade, the oldest scans fare worst — ARÚP 1930s grade 15 correct against 12 wrong, 1920s 11 against 8, while the 1950s and 1960s run 25:2 and 23:1. Those two worst decades are 1,108 lines out of 197,218, so they do not move the total; they are where to look first.

#### The cause, and the narrowing that answers it

Traced clause by clause on `1fca8eb` — **the thread's diagnosis is incomplete**. It attributes
the breakage to the triple-character clause reading `III` as a stutter. That reaches 5 of the 7
named lines; the full trace is:

| line                                                                                           | clause(s) that fire                       |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| `Sonda VIII/3`, `12.VIII.1977,`, `166. Hr.XLIII.1.`, `Lokalisace: I-VIII-eneol.II`, `w XVIII.` | vowel run **and** triple character        |
| `CCV. CCVI.`, `205; CCLXII).`                                                                  | **initial consonant geminate** (the `CC`) |

`I` is a vowel, so `III` is also a 3-vowel run. **Exempting the triple clause alone would fix
nothing** — the vowel-run clause still convicts `VIII`.

`_has_shape_garbage_evidence()` now carries a subtoken-level Roman-numeral exemption placed
**above all four clauses**, reusing the existing `_RE_ROMAN_TOKEN` (`^[IVXLCDM]{1,7}$`). Measured
here: **all 7 named broken lines stop firing, all 5 named fixed lines keep firing**, and every
positive the predicate's comment block names survives — `oueussd`, `cuxoaid`,
`IDIDIDIDIDIDUOID`, `NINNNIC`, `Tthts`, `rragment`, `vansasaasasa`, `lllll`.

Uppercase-only is deliberate: `RE_ROMAN_NUMERAL` accepts lowercase, and every letter of `lllll`
is a numeral glyph, so it would exempt real garbage. Known cost: a 7-glyph all-numeral stutter
(`DIDIDID`) is now exempt; the length cap keeps the real `IDIDIDIDIDIDUOID` convicted. Pinned in
`tests/test_text_utils.py::TestShapeGarbageWitness`.

**This narrows the predicate; it does not enable it.** `SHORT_GARBAGE_WITNESS_ENABLE` remains
`false`, the four constants stay in `_DELIBERATELY_NOT_TUNABLE`, and the flag-off suite is
unchanged (1038 → 1053 passing, the 15 new tests; 13 skipped / 2 xfailed either way). **The
12/12 measurement has not been re-run against the narrowed predicate** — that needs the 508
scored through `_rescore_row`, which needs the delivered batch. Until it is, the claim here is
only that the named broken class no longer fires, not that the flag is ready.

## 🚫 What this change is *not*

Two framings were tried and abandoned, and both are worth keeping on record because both survived review once.

**The symmetry argument does not hold.** The proposal was framed as "`rule_short_garbage` is the seventh sibling of six gated rules". Reading `50472fb`'s diff, it added `qs < thresh_trash` to sites that were *already* gated and never touched this rule. Those six live in §9, "explicit diagnostic hard gates" — late, weak, corroborating signals. `rule_short_garbage` sits in §6 among eight **early-exit** rules that all return unconditionally. It was never a guard that got skipped; it is a rule in the decisive family, which is a heavier burden, not a lighter one. The patch is not symmetric with those six anyway: they end in `check_rescues()`, this one returns `Trash` directly.

**"367,208 lifted / 0 demoted" is a theorem, not a measurement.** `determine_category` returns only `Empty`/`Trash`/`Noisy`/`Clear`, so removing an unconditional `return Trash` cannot demote anything. The direction was never in question. That figure is correct and carries **no evidence about whether the change is right** — only §3.2 does.

The first is the important one. **`vrstva` ("layer") is the commonest noun in archaeological field documentation**, and `vrstva 3` satisfies every condition of gate 6 at production signals — so a witness carrying the consonant-run clause would send clean Czech to `Trash`, which is the exact outcome this issue exists to prevent. Nothing is lost by dropping it: `sektlll` is reached by the triple-character test and `Tthts I` by the geminate test.

`detect_fused_words()` itself is left untouched for a separate reason: it feeds the `fused_ratio` term of the quality score and the `fused_words` CSV column, so narrowing it would move scores on every line in the corpus.

### Known false positives that remain

- **Latin/French/German loans with a 3+ vowel run** — `Poaceae`, `Naiade`, `Beaune`, `Radiouhlik`, `Sauerstoffflasche` (verified 5/7 above). Most carry `weird_ratio == 0.0` and so never reach gate 6 at all. That is a real margin but a **thin** one, because it depends on a signal outside the predicate. Measuring this class is a precondition for enabling the flag, not a follow-up.
- **Label-lexicon dropouts** — closing `_RE_NOTATION_LABELLED`'s label set narrowed `is_domain_notation()` from 30 of the 508 graded lines to **24**, and all six that dropped out land in `Trash`:

| line                  | `8fadeb9` | `f8ad4d8` | annotation |
|-----------------------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| `Orientace: SZ-JV`    | Clear     | **Trash** | Clear      |
| `Orientace: SV-JZ`    | Clear     | **Trash** | Clear      |
| `Komponenta: H`       | Noisy     | **Trash** | Noisy      |
| `XIV: 7`              | Noisy     | **Trash** | Noisy      |
| `XII: 2`              | Clear     | **Trash** | Clear      |
| `Bokalisace: B-XII-c` | Clear     | **Trash** | Noisy      |

  Five of the six were right before. `orientace` and `komponenta` are not in the 22-item set; `XIV` / `XII` satisfy the label shape without being words. Weighted agreement of upstream alone moves 19.9% → 17.3%. It does **not** reach the patch — `f8ad4d8 + patch` is identical to `8fadeb9 + patch` on all 508 lines.
- **Notation is not exempt from `rule_hard_sweep`**, deliberately. The predicate accepts >50% of *capitalised* dot-chained garbage (`Vvbn.Slaot.Vansas.` against `Reg.Bez.Aussig.`), so it cannot carry a hard-sweep exemption. A vowel-per-segment rule leaves ~40% and kills `Kr.Hr.`/`St.Pol.`; a short-segment majority leaves ~64%. Pinned by `TestNotationIsNotExemptFromHardSweep`.

### Out of reach by construction — needs a lexicon

`edelite`, `vfetennl k.`, `Slaot-o hiezazzt`. Phonotactically legal, so only word knowledge separates them from real vocabulary. Asserted as **part of the contract** in `test_the_disjunction_the_gate_will_evaluate_separates_the_population`, rather than quietly omitted — a lexicon-free approach has to own this residue, not pretend it away.

The honest form of the long-running claim is therefore: separating `malakofauna` from **`edelite`** needs a lexicon. Separating `malakofauna` from **`oueussd`** does not — that was the blocking claim for three rounds and it is false.

### Structural facts that constrain any future fix

- **No perplexity rule can fire at `wc ≤ 2`.** `SHORT_PPL_CAP` (850) < `HARD_SWEEP_PPL_MIN` (1,000) < `PPL_EXTREME_MIN` (3,000) < `PPL_GARBAGE_ABSOLUTE` (30,000). Perplexity is structurally unable to intervene at any value.
- **Section 7 (`rule_short_line`, `text_util.py:1380-1439`) is total for `wc ≤ 2`.** Every branch returns, so §8–§10 and `check_rescues()` are unreachable for that population. Its `damage` branch returns **`Noisy`, never `Trash`** (`text_util.py:1424-1425`) — which is why a witness placed there could never restore a `Trash` verdict.
- **`check_rescues()` (`text_util.py:1462`) cannot supply a `Noisy` floor.** All three of its floors — `_trailing_fill_rescued()`, `is_forgiven_headline()`, `is_clean_reference()` — evaluate `False` on all 11 lines tested, so it returns `Trash` for every one, `malakofauna` and `1 ks` included. Routing gate 6 through it buys symmetry and changes nothing.
- **`compute_valid_ratio()` (`text_util.py:2405`) is shape-only** — a token counts as valid on length ≥ 3, ≥ 70% alphabetic, no strange character, no mid-word uppercase. So `valid_word_ratio == 1.0` for this entire population, `oueussd` included. This is the suppression at the heart of the issue: section 7 already reads the discriminating witness inside its `damage` term and then vetoes it with `structurally_clean = valid_word_ratio >= 1.0`.
- **`rule_mid_uppercase` is unreachable**, shadowed by gate 7's identical `word_count <= 2` condition. Recorded in `UNREACHABLE_RULES` and pinned by `test_mid_uppercase_is_shadowed_by_short_line`.
- **Dot-chains still reach `Clear`** — periods collapse a line to `wc == 1`, the cap flattens perplexity, and gate 7 reads `valid_word_ratio >= 1.0` because `.` is in `ALLOWED_INTERNAL`. Same root cause as #30; deserves its own issue.
- **`service/text_api.py` double-imports its own app** — `uvicorn.run("text_api:app", ...)` re-runs the module body under a second name, so `app` / `_state` / `TextModelManager()` exist twice and the served `_state` is not the one `__main__` holds. Unrelated to #30; deserves its own issue.

### Harness divergence — the recurring failure mode

Three separate harnesses in this repository have fed `categorize_line` a value production never computes, and each produced confident wrong numbers before it was caught:

1. `tests/test_rotation_regression.py` fed the `LANG_SCORE_REMAP` cap (0.75) where production feeds the trust score (0.9163 on the `oueussd` fixture) — every assertion in the module was about a value that does not exist.
2. `_categ` / `_process_mocked_line` hardcoded `original_lang="ces_Latn"`, granting trust tier 1.0 where production applies `TRUST_TIER_UNKNOWN` (0.50). This is what made `Equus caballus` and `diapozitiv` look "green on master, therefore non-discriminating" — scored properly, all three are `Trash` and all three discriminate.
3. `tests/test_smoke.py` carried the same bug a third time, and additionally omitted `orig_lang_score` and `garbage_density`, leaving them at `1.0` / `0.0` and silently disabling `rule_hard_sweep`, `rule_extreme_ppl` and the density branch of `_has_strong_garbage_evidence`.

All three are fixed; `test_smoke.py` now routes through `_rescore_row`. `tools/short_garbage_witness_report.py` was described here as text-only *by construction* and therefore immune — and then produced the fourth instance anyway.

**4. `tools/short_garbage_witness_report.py` carried its own copy of the witness clauses.** It re-implemented all four so it could name which one fired, guarded by an assertion in `classify_line` that its diagnosis matched the predicate. The roman-numeral exemption (2026-09-10) landed in the predicate and not in the copy. The guard did exactly what it was written to do — it fired — on **0.74% of real lines**, which is every line carrying a roman numeral: `XVIII. '`, `23. VIII.1947.`, `580. Hr. VIII.4.`. The tool aborts on the first, so it was unusable on real data for precisely the exposure measurement the flag decision is gated on.

Fixed structurally rather than by patching the copy: `text_util.shape_garbage_clauses()` is now the single implementation, `_has_shape_garbage_evidence()` is `bool()` of it, and the report imports both the vocabulary and the logic. Verified over the full 367,208-line delivery: **0 crashes**. `tests/test_short_garbage_witness_report.py` gained the roman-numeral corpus and a test that fails if a local clause implementation reappears.

The lesson is the one this section already taught, with a sharper edge: *"text-only by construction"* was true and irrelevant. The bug was duplication, not signal reconstruction, and the guard caught it only because someone wrote the guard. The discipline is what prevents a fifth.

## ✅ What is settled

- **Perplexity cannot arbitrate this population**, and it runs backwards: FastText scores `oueussd` at 0.9163 and `malakofauna` at 0.56; with the cap lifted, real notation demotes at perplexity 3,000 while `oueussd` survives to 30,000. Recomputed values corroborate — `II/C` ≈ 6·10⁷ (correct) against `oueussd` ≈ 4,600 (garbage). No cap variant is separable (§3.5).
- **Notation is recoverable by shape**; vocabulary is not. `is_domain_notation()` handles grid refs, labelled refs, counts and abbreviation chains at 24 of 508 lines, 4.2% of the changed population.
- **`check_rescues()` cannot provide the middle state**, and section 7 — not gate 6 — is where 1–2 token lines are decided. **That is precisely why the witness does not belong there**: section 7 cannot return `Trash`.
- **No `wc`/character boundary isolates the errors** (§3.6). The question was asked and answered by measurement.
- **The suite can now see this class of change.** 814 → 891 → 954 passing; three golden edge cases that can actually see a gate on this rule; `_has_strong_garbage_evidence()` being `False` on the whole disputed population pinned on production vectors; a `Trash`-side counterpart to the swept-bounds guard.
