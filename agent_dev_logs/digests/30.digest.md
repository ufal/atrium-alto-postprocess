## 🎯 Goal & Core Constraint

The issue was opened as "the 2010+ documents are categorised badly", and the cause turned out to be one rule. `rule_short_garbage` (`text_util.py`, gate 5) returns `Trash` **unconditionally** for any 1–3 token line without Czech diacritics, weak language ID and any weirdness. It is the largest single Trash producer in the corpus — 6,333 firings, **28.4% of all Trash lines**, 62.5% on the two documents @DanaKriv flagged — and it concentrates on post-2000 documents precisely because those are tabular: one value per cell is exactly its firing profile.

The constraint is that nothing in the current feature set separates the two populations it acts on. At 1–2 tokens, `vansasaasasa` (garbage) and `malakofauna` (real) agree on valid-word ratio (1.000), weird ratio (0.350), garbage density (0.000), gibberish (0), ghost share (0) and raw quality score (0.647 vs 0.647). The decision is therefore a precision/recall trade, not a bug with a fix waiting to be found.

## 🏆 Headline Findings & Conclusions

**1. Perplexity is not a weak signal on this population — it is an inverted one.**
This is the finding that reframed the whole issue. Both surviving perplexity routes gate on *low* language-ID confidence, and on short lines FastText is confidently wrong in the wrong direction: `oueussd` scores 0.9163 and is garbage, `malakofauna` scores 0.56 and is real. Measured demotion thresholds with the cap lifted: real notation demotes at perplexity **3,000**, `oueussd` survives to **30,000**. @david-spacil's own recomputed values agree — `II/C` ≈ 6·10⁷ and correct, `1 ks` ≈ 5·10⁶ and correct, against `oueussd` ≈ 4,600 and garbage. Uncapping perplexity therefore punishes real archaeological notation roughly ten times sooner than the garbage it was aimed at.

**2. The `SHORT_PPL_CAP` hypothesis was mine, and measurement refuted it.**
The September round proposed raising or removing the cap so perplexity could reach the rules at `wc <= 2`, expecting the 15% wrong-lift rate to fall. It did not: no cap variant was separable from the patch (all CIs straddle zero), and per finding 1 the mechanism runs backwards. Two collateral facts came out of checking it — `validate_constants` **rejects** `SHORT_PPL_CAP` at 1001 or 3001 against the default `PERPLEXITY_THRESHOLD_MAX` of 1000, so those tested variants are not configurations the production tool will run; and for `wc <= 2` the delivered CSVs store the *capped* value, so true perplexity was never recoverable from the shipped data. That is what `perplex_raw` now fixes permanently.

**3. Every structural-protection predicate was inert on the affected population.**
`is_structured_line()` dispatches to seven predicates. All seven returned `False` on all 35 example lines from the thread — 245 evaluations, zero matches. `_RE_SIGLUM` caps abbreviation segments at four characters, which is why even `Reg.Bez.Aussig.` failed, on "Aussig". This produced `rule_domain_notation` in `8fadeb9`: a narrow, shape-based predicate for grid refs, counts and abbreviation chains, deliberately scoped to **notation, not vocabulary** — `malakofauna` and `Equus caballus` need a lexicon and are still gate 5's problem.

**4. `8fadeb9` shipped two features that could not change any outcome.**
@david-spacil measured `8fadeb9 + patch` as identical to `b1271a6 + patch` on all 508 annotated lines and diagnosed why: the predicate lived only in gate 5's guard, so the section-1 perplexity routes never saw it. Verifying that turned up a second, larger problem underneath — `apply_page_perplexity_blend()` re-scored through `score_line()`, which **re-applies `SHORT_PPL_CAP` to `wc <= 2`**, the blend's only target population. The blended number was pinned back to 850 before any rule saw it, so the blend could not move a category at *any* magnitude, and each row was left internally inconsistent: `perplex` held the blended value while `categ` had been computed at 850. No test caught it, because the blend tests assert on the blended *number*, never on a category it moved.

**5. An adversarial review overturned two of the three first-round fixes, and was right on both.**
`_RE_NOTATION_ABBR` was `^(?:[A-Za-z]{1,8}\.){2,6}$` — it constrained shape but not content, so `kfjs.qmwx.zzpl.vvbn.` matched as readily as `Reg.Bez.Aussig.` Measured on dot-chains built from this corpus's own garbage vocabulary: **100% accepted**. Requiring a capital initial per segment takes that to **0%** with all eleven pinned notation shapes preserved, and loses nothing lowercase — `č.neg.`, `č.j.`, `s.j.` and `inv.č.` all match `_RE_SIGLUM`, so `is_structured_line()` already keeps them out of gate 5. Separately, `rule_hard_sweep` should never have been exempted: unlike the other two routes it also requires `orig_lang_score < HARD_SWEEP_LANG_MAX`, an independent second witness. Exempting all three let 65% of dot-mutated garbage escape to Noisy/Clear; leaving hard sweep armed trashed 600 of 600 at no cost to notation.

**6. A pre-existing bug was found that is not this issue's, but shares its root cause.**
At the shipped configuration, on pristine `8fadeb9` with no patch applied, `kfjs qmwx zzpl vvbn` is `Trash` while `kfjs.qmwx.zzpl.vvbn.` is **`Clear`**. Four periods collapse the line to `wc == 1`, `SHORT_PPL_CAP` flattens its perplexity below every route, and gate 7 reads `valid_word_ratio >= 1.0` and returns Clear — `ALLOWED_INTERNAL` contains `.`, so `compute_valid_ratio` scores dot-chains as fully valid. This is issue #30's own root cause in a different disguise: **there is no Trash route at `wc <= 2` for a line whose only defect is that its tokens are not words.** It deserves its own issue.

**7. The parked fixtures question rests on a harness artifact.**
@david-spacil's table showed `Equus caballus` and `diapozitiv` as green-on-master and therefore non-discriminating. But `_process_mocked_line` and `test_calibration._categ` both hardcode `original_lang="ces_Latn"`, giving trust tier 1.0, where production sends `isl`/`ast`/`ron` through `TRUST_TIER_UNKNOWN = 0.50` for trust scores of 0.28 / 0.385 / 0.485 — all under `LANG_SCORE_REMAP`. Scored the way production scores them, **all three are Trash on `8fadeb9`** and all three discriminate. This is the same class of harness bug already fixed once in `test_rotation_regression.py`.

## 🏁 Final Bottom Line

The direction is right and @DanaKriv's verdict on a full recompute carries weight, but the patch is not yet ready to take as-is and the reasons have changed since September. What is settled: perplexity cannot arbitrate this population, the notation subset can be recovered by shape, and both `8fadeb9` features now actually function. What is not: `rule_short_garbage`'s core trade — roughly 26,000 garbage lines reaching `Clear` against a 4.7 : 1 improvement ratio — remains a judgment call that only a lexicon or phonotactic signal can dissolve, and `tools/quality_model/` (issue #23) is the only thing in the repo that plausibly could.

Three questions are with @david-spacil and block his PR: whether "go ahead and integrate" means open it against `test` now and whether both collections should be recomputed on the new baseline first (yes to both — the 378,735 figure no longer describes the code); how to resolve the red `oueussd` test (recommend `strict=True` xfail keeping `Trash` as the goal, verified to give `769 passed … 1 xfailed` with the patch and `XPASS(strict)` without it, rather than freezing `Clear` as correct); and the fixtures format (none of the three offered options — see finding 7).
