ALTO XML Files Postprocessing Pipeline

split · stats · extract · classify · aggregate

Post-processing of OCR output — ALTO XML, and also PAGE XML, hOCR, PDF, office and text files — into extracted text and a scored line-quality table, plus per-page ALTO.

Deserialisation of ALTO and every other OCR or text-bearing input, and OCR quality control. The fan-out point of the whole pipeline.
